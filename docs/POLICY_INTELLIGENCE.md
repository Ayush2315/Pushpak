# Project PUSHPAK — Policy Intelligence & Decision-Support Framework

## 1. Architectural Mission
The **Policy Intelligence Engine** (`backend/analytics/policy_intelligence.py`) forms Milestone 3 of Project PUSHPAK. Its core objective is to synthesize low-level fare observations, flight registry metadata, route network metrics, and volatility distributions into **transparent, explainable, and auditable policy decision-support intelligence** for civil aviation analysts and economic policymakers.

## 2. Strict Data Honesty & Statutory Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Statutory Non-Regulatory Notice**:
> All priority classifications (`HIGH_ATTENTION`, `MONITOR`, `LOW_ATTENTION`) and flags generated by PUSHPAK are **analytical decision-support heuristics** engineered for evaluation and research. They are **NOT official statutory designations, regulatory warnings, or DGCA/MoCA government mandates**.

1. **Observed Dataset Records**: Metric counts represent cumulative dataset entries across time, not confirmed daily air traffic control frequencies.
2. **Simulation Transparency**: All prototype fare observations are explicitly tagged with `data_mode="demo_simulation"` and `environment="offline"`. They are never presented as live market quotes.
3. **Zero Probabilistic AI**: Classification and insight generation are 100% deterministic and rule-based. Zero LLMs, neural networks, or external AI APIs are utilized.

## 3. Policy Priority Classification Logic

Every air corridor is evaluated against three quantifiable economic dimensions:
1. **Price Dispersion / Volatility**: Evaluated using the Coefficient of Variation ($CV$).
2. **Advance Purchase Premium**: Spread between walk-up ($T+1$) and advance ($T+45$) booking horizons.
3. **Inter-Carrier Dispersion & Market Competition**: Rupee spread between carriers and number of observed active airlines.

### Priority Category Assignment Rules

| Priority Category | Severity Score | Analytical Rule Conditions | Policy Implication |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`HIGH_ATTENTION`** | **`3`** | Triggered if **ANY** of the following hold:<br>• $CV > 30.0\%$ (High Volatility)<br>• $\text{Walk-Up Premium} > 60.0\%$ (Severe Surge)<br>• $\text{Carrier Spread} > 35.0\%$ **AND** Active Carriers $< 3$ | Prime candidate for tariff transparency scrutiny and consumer fare-shock review. |
| **`MONITOR`** | **`2`** | Triggered if **ANY** of the following hold (and not High Attention):<br>• $15.0\% \le CV \le 30.0\%$ (Moderate Yield Dynamics)<br>• $25.0\% \le \text{Walk-Up Premium} \le 60.0\%$<br>• $15.0\% \le \text{Carrier Spread} \le 35.0\%$ | Standard dynamic airline revenue management; routine observation recommended. |
| **`LOW_ATTENTION`** | **`1`** | Triggered when **ALL** metrics are below baseline thresholds:<br>• $CV < 15.0\%$<br>• $\text{Walk-Up Premium} < 25.0\%$<br>• $\text{Carrier Spread} < 15.0\%$ | High price stability and predictable inter-carrier benchmarks. |

## 4. Quantitative Policy Flags

The platform emits four standardized, numbers-traceable policy flags:

### 1. `HIGH_VOLATILITY`
- **Trigger**: $CV > 30.0\%$ (Severity: `HIGH`) or $15.0\% \le CV \le 30.0\%$ (Severity: `MEDIUM`).
- **Explanation**: Reports exact $CV$ percentage, sample standard deviation ($s$), and mean fare ($\mu$).

### 2. `HIGH_WALKUP_PREMIUM`
- **Trigger**: $\text{Markup} > 60.0\%$ (Severity: `HIGH`) or $25.0\% \le \text{Markup} \le 60.0\%$ (Severity: `MEDIUM`).
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Walk-Up Premium (\%)} = \left( \frac{\overline{\text{Fare}}_{T+1} - \overline{\text{Fare}}_{T+45}}{\overline{\text{Fare}}_{T+45}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Explanation**: Quantifies consumer penalty for last-minute bookings compared to advance planning.

### 3. `LIMITED_OBSERVED_COMPETITION`
- **Trigger**: Number of observed operating airlines $\le 2$ (Severity: `MEDIUM`), or $\le 1$ (Severity: `HIGH`).
- **Benchmark**: Competitive corridor threshold is defined as $\ge 3$ distinct carriers.

### 4. `SIGNIFICANT_PRICE_SPREAD`
- **Trigger**: Spread between highest and lowest carrier average fare $> 35.0\%$ (Severity: `HIGH`) or $> 15.0\%$ (Severity: `MEDIUM`).
- **Explanation**: Reports the exact rupee difference ($\Delta \text{₹}$) between competing carriers.

## 5. REST API Specifications

### 1. Route Policy Assessment
- **Endpoint**: `GET /api/v1/policy/routes/{route_code}`
- **Example**: `GET /api/v1/policy/routes/DEL-BOM`
- **Response**: `RoutePolicyAssessment`
  - Includes `priority_classification`, `volatility_cv`, `walkup_premium_pct`, `carrier_price_spread`, `flags`, and `provenance`.

### 2. Network Macro Policy Overview
- **Endpoint**: `GET /api/v1/policy/network`
- **Response**: `NetworkPolicyOverview`
  - Aggregates total corridors monitored, priority distributions, corridors sorted by volatility and walk-up surge, and flag breakdown.

### 3. Query Active Policy Flags
- **Endpoint**: `GET /api/v1/policy/flags`
- **Parameters**:
  - `severity` (optional): `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`, `INFO`
  - `route_code` (optional): e.g. `DEL-BOM`
- **Response**: `PolicyFlagsResponse`
